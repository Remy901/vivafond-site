Privacy policy

In short: Vivafond collects no personal data.

  • No data collected. No account, nothing sent over the Internet, no analytics, no ads.
  • Cursor position. Read only to animate the wallpaper on your Mac. Never stored or transmitted.
  • Settings. Your preferences (wallpaper, favorites, smoothness) are stored locally on your Mac.
  • Suggest an idea. Opens your mail app with a pre-filled email. Nothing is sent without your action; emails are only used to reply to you.
