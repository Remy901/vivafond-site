FAQ

How do I change the wallpaper?
Open Vivafond, hover a card and click "Apply", or use the cat icon in the menu bar.
The wallpaper doesn't react to my mouse.
Make sure "Reacts to the mouse" is on at the top of the window. The wallpaper reacts when the cursor moves over the desktop.
My Mac gets warm or the battery drains.
Pick "Battery saver" for Animation and avoid wallpapers marked "Demanding" on battery. The wallpaper pauses when hidden.
How do I remove the animated wallpaper?
Menu bar cat icon, then "Remove wallpaper". Your usual wallpaper comes back.
